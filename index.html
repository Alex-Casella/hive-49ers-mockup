What changed in this mockup

  1. Every dataset shows where it came from and how old it is.
  2. Each unit shows Full strength beside This week, plus the exact values. The headline is rounded; the exact numbers explain why −1.7 can show as 84 → 82 or 79 → 78.
  3. Why? lists only the players who moved the number, each with their own share. The shares add up to the total, so no player is shown next to the whole unit's drop.
  4. Injured backups behind a healthy starter go in a No effect group with the reason. C.J. West is here.
  5. Your sit-outs are a separate step (Reported → With your sit-outs) with their own undo, so a simulation never reads as news.
  6. Roster rows show status, body part and how old the update is. Old Q/Out updates get an amber "may be out of date" tag.
  7. The player sheet separates three dates: official report history, the provider's last update, and when HIVE last checked. Missing facts say "not provided".
  8. Proposal: switch the rating to Madden only. Today's "PFF" input is generated (Madden × 0.95 + noise), not real PFF. Toggle to compare.
